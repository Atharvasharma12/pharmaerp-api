The ERP system follows a Workspace → Company → Branch hierarchy. A Workspace represents a customer organization (pharmacy business) and serves as the top-level tenant. Within a workspace, the customer can create one or more Companies, and each company can manage multiple Branches. Users belong to a workspace and can be granted access to specific companies and branches based on their roles and permissions. Company-level data includes business information such as GST number, drug licenses, trade licenses, PAN, tax configurations, billing settings, and other statutory or compliance-related details, since these are shared across all branches of that company. Branch-level data includes inventory, stock, batches, purchases, sales, POS operations, and day-to-day pharmacy activities, as stock and transactions are maintained separately for each branch. We are building a multi-tenant Retail Pharmacy ERP SaaS platform that enables customers to centrally manage companies, branches, users, compliance, inventory, sales, purchases, billing, subscriptions, and reporting within a scalable and secure architecture.
